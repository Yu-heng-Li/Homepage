01

Background背景

My training is in medical technology. I have worked on brain–computer interfaces, deep brain stimulation and flow cytometry — instruments that read and write the body at the scale of neurons and cells.

Those systems taught me two things. The hardest part of health data is rarely the sensor; it is whether a person can understand, trust and carry what was measured. And most of the value disappears once the measurement is locked inside a lab, a hospital, or a vendor's database.

So I now build in between: AI-assisted tools that turn clinical documents into something a patient can actually read, and decentralized primitives that let people hold their own records. I write code as a way of testing an argument — small, honest prototypes rather than decks.

我的背景是医疗技术，做过脑机接口、脑深部电刺激（DBS）和流式细胞术——这些仪器在神经元与细胞的尺度上读取并干预人体。

它们教给我两件事。健康数据最难的部分往往不是传感器，而是一个人能否理解、信任并随身带走被测量出来的东西；而一旦数据被锁在实验室、医院或厂商的数据库里，大部分价值就消失了。

所以我现在做两者之间的工作：用 AI 把临床文档变成患者真的能读懂的形态，用去中心化原语让人真正持有自己的记录。我写代码是为了检验论点——要小而诚实的原型，不要 PPT。

04

How I build我怎么做事

  1. Never fabricate what the record doesn't say. In Porcelain Time most ceramics survive only as date ranges. Rather than invent a birthday, the interface labels every match — verified historical date, nearest event, thematic — and admits which one you got.

    记录没说的事，绝不编造。「瓷时」里大多数瓷器只留下年代区间。与其编一个生日，不如在界面上标明这次的匹配属于哪一种——同日事件、相邻事件，还是主题关联。

  2. Privacy is a build constraint, not a feature. Keys stay server-side, database writes from the browser are denied, row-level security is tested with two distinct users, and only synthetic data is collected until real-data prerequisites pass review.

    隐私是约束条件，不是卖点。密钥只留在服务端，浏览器端写入一律拒绝，行级权限用两个不同用户实测，在真实数据的准入条件通过之前只收集合成数据。

  3. Bilingual from the data model up. English and Chinese are typed dictionaries read by one component tree, with tests that keep the keys aligned — not a second copy of the app that quietly drifts.

    双语从数据模型长出来。中英文是同一棵组件树读取的两套带类型词库，并有测试保证键名对齐——而不是一份会悄悄跑偏的副本。
